PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how BCA Study Portal handles information and third-party technologies when you use our educational website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Policy

BCA Study Portal is an educational website providing study notes, practical programming resources, assignments, case studies and other learning materials for students.

This policy describes the general privacy practices applicable to visitors of the website.

2. Information We Collect

BCA Study Portal does not currently require visitors to create an account or submit personal information in order to access the educational content on the website.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the study portal.

If communication or other features are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain the information collected and how it is used.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information that may become available through website operation or third-party services may be used for purposes such as maintaining the website, improving user experience, understanding website performance and protecting the website from misuse.

We do not sell visitors' personal information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies when required by third-party services integrated into the website.

For example, third-party services such as Google services may use cookies or similar technologies depending on how those services are configured and used on the website.

Visitors can control or manage cookies through their browser settings. Some website features or third-party services may not function as intended when cookies are disabled.

5. Google Services

BCA Study Portal may use Google services such as Google Translate, Google Search Console and, in the future, Google AdSense.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

When advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Google may also use advertising cookies to personalize ads based on users' visits to this or other websites, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Users can manage Google's advertising personalization through Google's Ads Settings.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

The website may contain links to external websites, learning resources, videos or other third-party services.

BCA Study Portal does not control the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

This website provides general educational content for BCA students. Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through any website feature unless it is specifically requested for a legitimate purpose.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to maintain the security and integrity of the website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please visit our Contact Us page.
