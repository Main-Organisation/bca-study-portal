FLOWCHART / PROCESS IMAGES:
A flowchart must follow standard rules for direction, symbols, and connections to remain clear and easy to read. [1, 2]  
Direction and Flow 

• Top to bottom: Flow should generally move from top to bottom or left to right. 
• Use arrows: Connect every shape with arrow lines to show the exact path of the process. 
• No crossing lines: Keep lines neat and avoid letting arrows cross or overlap each other. [1, 3, 4]  

Standard Symbols 

• Oval (Terminator): Marks the start and end points of a process. 
• Rectangle (Process): Represents actions, tasks, or calculations. 
• Diamond (Decision): Shows a choice point that branches into paths like "Yes" and "No". 
• Parallelogram (Input/Output): Indicates data entering or leaving the system. [1, 4, 5]  

Core Rules 

• Single start: Every chart needs a clear start point. 
• Labeled branches: Label every exit path from a decision diamond clearly. 
• Keep it simple: Limit the chart to one page and remove unnecessary details. [1, 4]
